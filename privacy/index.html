FlightLine Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

FlightLine collects only what it needs to identify the aircraft you photograph, log your sightings and run your account, and it never sells your data or shows ads.

FlightLine: Plane Spotting (“FlightLine”, “we”) is a plane-spotting app and community published by Joseph Bultena. This policy covers the FlightLine iOS app, including beta versions distributed through TestFlight. Questions go to joe.bultena@gmail.com.

Photo date and locationWhen the photo carries them, or from your location at the moment you take it in the appTo place a sighting at the nearest airport and award points

We never ask for your contacts, and we never track your location in the background.

Photo identification happens only when you make a card. Lookups of aircraft photos send no personal information about you.

Sharing, storage and security

  • No selling, no ads. We don’t sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for tracking across other apps.
  • What others see. Your display name and points may appear on leaderboards. Cards and photos stay private unless you share them yourself.
  • Where it lives. Most of your data is stored on your iPhone. Data you sync is stored with Supabase. Photos sent for identification are used to answer that request, not to build a photo library.
  • How long. We keep your account data until you delete your account. Per-device request counts used for rate limiting are kept only as long as needed to enforce daily limits.
  • Security. Data travels over encrypted connections, access to our database is restricted per user, and our service keys are never stored in the app.
  • Legal requests. We disclose data only when required by law.

Your choices

  • Permissions. Camera, photo library and location access are optional and can be changed anytime in iPhone Settings. Without them you can still browse the app, and photos without a location simply earn no points.
  • Delete your account. Go to Account in the app and tap Delete account. This removes your account and the data we store on our servers. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone.
  • Access or questions. Email joe.bultena@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to request a copy or correction.
  • Children. FlightLine isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
